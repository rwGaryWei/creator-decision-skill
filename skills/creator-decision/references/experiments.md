# Experiments that inform the next decision

Name the uncertain assumption, material/version, participant source (if any), task, observation, resource ceiling, interpretation rule, and next decision. Distinguish a plan from a completed result. The user's actual constraints override generic validation playbooks.

Choose the cheapest test that can meaningfully change the investment. Internal consistency, a creator walkthrough or a comparison of drafts can be worthwhile when audiences are unavailable; none proves external acceptance. Do not silently assume access to dozens of participants, paid traffic or production-ready media.

If the recommendation is to build a smaller work, supply its concrete design before its test. For a tool, specify input, user actions, result and recovery; for a video, the opening, explanatory or emotional progression and payoff; for a drama, the scene, character choice and consequence; for a game, the loop, rules, feedback and end condition. Use the creator's actual idea rather than a generic checklist. Proposed scope is a design suggestion, not a measured optimum or a promise of completion within an unverified estimate.

Keep variables comparable when possible. If comparing video openings, hold the topic/body constant; if assessing a tool, measure setup and correction as well as completion; if assessing a drama, record why a character choice was understood; if assessing a game, examine consequences and voluntary continued play.

State conditions for continue/change/pause before looking at results. Thresholds are agreed project heuristics, not universal industry standards. Inconclusive results may mean the experiment was weak or the sample mismatched. A negative result does not establish the creator's whole idea is worthless.

Synthetic perspectives can generate objections or questions. Label them conspicuously, keep them separate from real feedback, and do not count simulated people as participants. Do not invent recruitment, sample sizes, completion, retention, revenue or consent.

If the work is formal human-participant research, prepare the protocol and confirm applicable institutional requirements before recruitment. Ordinary internal artifact checks can continue. External contact and publication follow actual user authorization.
