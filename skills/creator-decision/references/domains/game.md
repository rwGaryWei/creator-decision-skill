# Games

Find the intended player experience and the loop: what the player repeatedly does, decides, sees/hears as feedback, learns, and chooses next. Assess content volume only through its contribution to that experience.

An idea review examines meaningful choices, understandable consequences, challenge, progression, replay motivation, and production feasibility. A cozy relationship game and a competitive action game need different criteria. Do not force monetization onto a learning or expressive project.

For a build, record the exact version and actions actually played. For footage, record only observed events and avoid first-person claims about control feel. Store descriptions and reviews can identify hypotheses but cannot replace play. Code inspection is not execution.

Separate gameplay, technical reliability, writing/art/voice execution, value expectations, and principled attitudes toward AI. “AI content exists” is not a demonstrated cause of dislike. Public review samples need language, date, sort, sample size, selection method, and version limitations.

Offer changes that preserve the intended experience. A thousand NPCs may be reduced to a few consequential relationships; conversely, an already clear and engaging loop with sufficient production capacity may justify more breadth. Explain the evidence and what would change the recommendation.

Example experiment: a short playable loop with a consequential choice. Observe whether a player can describe its consequence, distinguish characters, recover from confusion, and voluntarily try a different approach. A creator walkthrough checks coherence, not external fun or retention.

AI tradeoffs: asset selection and consistency, localization and voice corrections, executable-code reliability, authoring and testing narrative branches, runtime moderation/cost only when runtime generation actually exists. AI-assisted production does not imply live AI NPCs.

Legacy Steam evidence is optional. A game need not be on Steam and no other domain should need an AppID. Retain original sample provenance; do not redistribute raw third-party reviews without checking permission.
