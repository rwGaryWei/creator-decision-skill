# Decision card

**Decision now:** What investment are we deciding about?

**Recommendation:** Continue / expand / narrow / change / test / pause [specific investment], under [conditions].

**Preserve:** The intended value, expression, or experience.

**Why:** The decisive supporting and opposing reasons. Identify source/observation, inference, and unknowns.

**Options and tradeoffs:** What each meaningful alternative keeps, changes, might improve, and costs.

**Next experiment:** Assumption; feasible task; material; observation; resource ceiling; interpretation rule.

**What would change this advice:** Evidence or goal change.

**Observation limits:** What was and was not inspected.

**Your decision:** Pending until you actually accept, modify, reject, or defer.

Detailed sources, assumptions and version history can follow when useful.
