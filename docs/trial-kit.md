# Creator trial kit · draft, not a completed study

Purpose: learn whether a creator can obtain and understand a useful next decision with this alpha. Four early trials spanning the four domains would provide varied usability feedback, not a representative market sample. Participants have not been recruited. Contact or publication requires explicit authorization; research use needs the relevant institutional process checked first.

## Session plan

Allow roughly 30–45 minutes per willing adult creator. This is a planning estimate. Ask them to bring their own small decision and only material they may share. Avoid collecting sensitive personal data or requiring confidential work. The facilitator should observe first, not rescue every difficulty; record interventions.

1. Explain that the tool is experimental and may be wrong. Participation is voluntary, questions can be skipped, and they may stop. Agree on note/recording and quotation permissions separately.
2. Record the goal, medium, stage and what they want to preserve in their own words.
3. Ask them to install/use the skill using the public instructions and choose a real next decision.
4. Observe where they hesitate, need help, or misinterpret the output. Record exact version and access limitations.
5. Ask them to explain the recommendation and what would change it. Do not ask leading questions such as “Wasn't this helpful?”
6. Ask what they accept, modify, reject or defer and why. Do not infer acceptance from politeness.
7. Ask which suggested action they could actually perform. If they later perform it, collect results separately with permission.

## Neutral interview prompts

- What were you trying to decide before this session?
- Which part, if any, changed how you see that decision?
- What does the recommendation ask you to do next?
- What felt missing, generic, too confident or inconsistent with your goal?
- What would you do without the skill?
- What should we change before you would use it again?

## Session record

Use a pseudonymous ID. Store date, domain, stage, host/model, skill version, consent scope, task, completion with/without assistance, time, observed obstacles, participant's own judgment and facilitator interpretations separately. Include failures. Keep recordings and identifiable notes private, minimize retention, and agree a deletion/withdrawal process before collecting them. A public summary requires separate permission for identifiable quotations.

Internal rehearsals are allowed but labeled author/internal. Synthetic personas can improve interview questions; they cannot fill participant rows. The proposed early release gate of at least three of four independent creators completing a review without intervention is a product choice, not proof of effectiveness. If no external creators are available, retain alpha status and report that limitation.
