Primary pages inspected on 2026-09-25. This is a description-level comparison, not a hands-on performance ranking or an exhaustive novelty search. External pages may change.

| Project | What its own material describes | Implication for this project |
|---|---|---|
| [validate-idea-skills](https://github.com/elcodabra/validate-idea-skills) | A staged commercial idea-validation workflow covering demand, competitors, smoke tests, pre-sales and a go/no-go decision | “An AI that challenges ideas” is already an occupied space. Our proposed emphasis is cross-media creative goals, work-level revision and evidence-aware reassessment. |
| [gstack office-hours](https://github.com/garrytan/gstack/blob/main/office-hours/SKILL.md) | Separate startup diagnostics and builder brainstorming, including learning and open-source projects | Respecting noncommercial goals is not novel by itself. We need to demonstrate domain-specific usefulness and reliable follow-up rather than claim first invention. |
| [Synthetic Users](https://www.syntheticusers.com/) | AI participant interviews for exploration and concept testing; its site also says real user research remains essential | Simulation may suggest questions. We do not count simulated responses as real acceptance and have not verified the vendor's accuracy claims. |

The research opportunity is a testable combination: actionable creative advice, preservation of the user's goal, factual/evidence discipline, stability across confidence framing, and appropriate updates when evidence changes. Whether this combination helps more than a well-written ordinary prompt is unanswered. The B1 baseline deliberately includes many of the same good principles.

Avoid promotional claims such as “nobody has built this”, “eliminates bias”, or “predicts whether the market will approve”. A future literature review should document search terms, databases, dates, inclusion rules and near alternatives; this short comparison does not establish publication novelty.
