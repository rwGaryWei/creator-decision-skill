# Owner-assisted product review

Decision date: 2026-09-25. This is an internal product-design review, not an independent evaluation or a new model experiment.

## Agreed route

Improve practical Skill use before starting formal research. The first review is planned for 30–60 minutes with the owner; elapsed review time has not been measured. No independent reviewer is currently available. The existing four domains and three entry stages remain the implementation scope.

This resolves the immediate route choice in TSK-036: continue product revision and defer formal research. It does not freeze a formal sample size or satisfy the calibration dependency in TSK-035. Before any later formal run, freeze the design, budget, cases, analysis and reviewer arrangements, allowing a result that shows no advantage.

## First preference and resulting changes

On an explicitly authored gardening-game exercise, the owner selected a brief initial response with detail available on request. In response to the follow-up about actionability, the owner requested an actual minimum-version design grounded in the creator's existing idea, rather than only advice to make a smaller version. These are product requirements, not quality scores or evidence that the owner has carried out the suggested action.

The Skill and card template now make this default explicit. A short answer must still disclose any decisive objection or uncertainty. The existing gardening prototype demonstration now offers a paper rule check when no playable build is available, reserving implementation and player-experience claims for actual inspection.

When a smaller version is recommended, the answer must also provide its concrete experience, flow or sequence, scope and completion boundary. An [authored gardening design](../examples/game/minimum-slice.md) illustrates this requirement; proposed rules are identified separately from the original brief. After reading it, the owner clarified the desired combination: give the macro advice first, then the detailed plan. This supersedes any interpretation that essential design details should wait for a second request. It is a response-structure decision, not acceptance of the illustrative game's rules or a rating of real gameplay.

These edits have not been evaluated in fresh model sessions. The earlier 36 pilot outputs remain unchanged and do not measure these revisions.

## Advisory role and comparable works

The owner clarified that when a creator's preferred design appears to conflict with their goal, the Skill should search online for works with comparable themes or pacing, assess the relevant factors together, and explain advantages, disadvantages and questions. It must not decide for the author. This completes the protected-principle review for TSK-001 alongside the already chosen four domains and three stages.

Concrete minimum-version designs remain proposed options, preserving the earlier requirement. The macro opening is an assessment rather than a binding verdict. The new comparison guidance records inspected scope, similarities, consequential differences, sources and limits; it separates comprehension problems from dislike of pacing and comparison from causal proof. No actual comparable short drama has been researched in this requirements discussion, and no new search result or viewing is claimed.

Future behavioral check: use a non-test development case where the creator wants to preserve a quiet style while reaching a broad audience and feedback concerns unclear motivation. Check actual retrieval and source support, comparison relevance, consideration of resources and audience, concrete alternatives and retention of the author's decision. Fail invented references, unsupported claims of viewing, treating one popular work as proof, assuming faster pacing is the solution, or recording a choice the creator never made. With unavailable retrieval, require an explicit gap and a useful analysis limited to supplied material. Status: planned, not passed.

## Checks for the new minimum-version requirement

Status: planned; no behavioral pass is claimed. Use development material, not held-out formal cases. For each domain, review whether the actual answer:

1. Starts with a macro recommendation and then converts the supplied idea into a concrete flow or creative sequence with enough rules or content to begin work in the same answer; generic advice to build a prototype or withholding essential design until another request fails this check.
2. Preserves the stated core and distinguishes supplied requirements from new design suggestions; unsupported audience claims or an unannounced goal change fail it.
3. States included and omitted scope, a feasible first deliverable, and a completion boundary. An internal consistency check is not presented as the finished artifact or market validation.

Include a countercase where the existing work is already appropriately scoped; automatic shrinking should fail. Record exact input, Skill version, output and reviewer role before assigning a result. The owner exercise may reveal requirement gaps but does not substitute for these future behavior checks.
