# Creator Decision evaluation report · preparation stage

## Abstract

This repository implements a four-domain creative-decision skill with optional evidence and versioning tools. Engineering checks and fictional demonstration materials exist. No comparative model experiment or independent creator study has been completed. Consequently this report makes no claim that the skill improves decisions, predicts market acceptance or reduces sycophancy.

## Artifact and motivation

AI-assisted production can make it easier to create drafts. The project addresses a separate question: which next investment and revision is justified by the creator's goal and available evidence? The alpha combines a short decision card, domain-specific reasoning, explicit observation limits, alternative changes and practical experiments. See [scope](scope.md) and [architecture](architecture.md).

## Related work

An initial primary-source comparison identifies adjacent idea-validation skills, builder brainstorming workflows and simulated-user research tools. It does not establish novelty or comparative superiority. See [related work](related-work.md).

## Method and materials

The [draft protocol](../evaluations/PROTOCOL.md) proposes B0, B1 and S comparisons with matched cases and fixed model settings. A 36-run pilot would precede a selected 180- or 432-response formal design over 24 authored projects. Cases, prompts, scoring anchors and raw-output preservation rules are supplied. Formal candidate materials are not frozen and independent reviewers are not confirmed.

## Results currently available

The deterministic checks recorded in [validation](validation.md) cover local engineering behavior. Twelve fictional output examples illustrate intended use. Model comparisons completed: **0**. Independent creator sessions completed: **0**. Independent human ratings: **0**. Effect sizes, confidence intervals, agreement and market outcomes: **not estimated**.

## Discussion and limitations

Implementation and testable materials make future investigation possible, but do not answer the research question. Important limitations include author-created public cases, lack of actual interactive/multimedia assessment, shared-model dependence, incomplete blinding, uncertain reviewer resources and absent outcome ground truth. A literal citation check cannot establish semantic support. A version-bound local journal is not authenticated consent.

## Next decision

Select an authorized run environment and budget, review/freeze materials, execute the pilot, repair issues on non-test data, and decide whether independent review is feasible. If those resources are unavailable, publish only the alpha engineering artifact and this preparation-stage report. A completed research report requires actual methods, observations and limitations to replace—not merely fill decoratively—these missing results.

No participants have been recruited, no institutional approval is claimed, and no manuscript has been submitted.
